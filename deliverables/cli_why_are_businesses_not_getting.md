# Tamil Reel Script: Why are businesses not getting leads from their websites?

## 1. Topic & Audience
- **Topic:** Why are businesses not getting leads from their websites?
- **Audience:** General Tamil content audience
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Tone:** Tanglish
- **Objective:** awareness
- **Assumed Defaults:** platform = Instagram Reels

## 2. Research Summary
### Key Findings:
- 96% of website visitors are not ready to buy on their first visit, meaning hard-sell pages fail to convert cold traffic.
- 80% of new leads generated never convert into a sale, highlighting a breakdown in lead nurturing and friction during the conversion process.
- Websites frequently attract visitors but fail to generate enquiries due to hidden Call-to-Actions (CTAs), weak trust signals, poor user experience (UX), and unclear messaging.

- **Chosen Content Angle:** Shift from 'Brochure Website' to 'Lead Engine': Fixing the 3 Hidden Friction Points Blocking Conversions
- **Why Chosen:** Directly targets business owners who waste ad spend bringing traffic to a site that lacks clear CTAs, trust signals, and nurturing hooks, giving them immediate actionable clarity.
